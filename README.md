## 🎮 About the Game

Grail is a fantasy autobattler deckbuilder developed and published by Sokpop Collective. It released on September 1, 2026, for PC and macOS. The game combines roguelite progression, automated card battles, PvE encounters, asynchronous PvP, and character-based deckbuilding.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Grail**. This trainer enhances Grail's automated deckbuilding runs by providing control over progression resources, card development, unlocks, and battle-related advantages. It is designed around the game's card upgrades, enchantments, traits, trophies, characters, and automated combat systems.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Grail does not use conventional character movement as its primary gameplay system. Progress is handled through menus, deck decisions, events, upgrades, and automated encounters. Use the trainer after entering a run and before confirming major deck or progression actions.

### Tools & Inventory

The main inventory is the player's card collection and active deck. Cards can be collected, upgraded, enchanted, and modified with stickers to create cascading effects. Resource options should be activated before making purchases or applying card improvements.

### World & Environment

A run typically lasts between 15 and 45 minutes and moves through a sequence of encounters, rewards, upgrades, and special events. PvE battles are fought against monsters, while asynchronous PvP allows prepared decks to compete against other players. Trophies earned from victories contribute to character and cosmetic progression.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is fully automated. Once the deck is prepared, cards are played by the game and their traits, upgrades, enchantments, and synergies determine the outcome. Trainer options are best used before combat begins to test deck interactions or provide controlled advantages during a run.
